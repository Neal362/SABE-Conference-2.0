SABE 2026
Early-Career Researchers Workshop
  • Early-Career Researchers (ECR) Workshop - June 22, 2026
  • ECR Workshop offers PhD students and early postdocs an opportunity to present their work and receive valuable feedback and advice on both their papers and presentations from experienced scholars. The top paper presented at the workshop will be awarded the John Tomer Memorial Prize. This prize, presented by SABE, recognizes outstanding research presented at the ECR Workshop.
  • Who is eligible?.
  • PhD students and postdocs (within 2 years of PhD).
